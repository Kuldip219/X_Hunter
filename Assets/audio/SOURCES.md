## Music — original work (public domain)

`gameplay_music.wav` is **original work generated for this project** and
is released into the public domain (no attribution required). It is a
132 BPM, 8-bar chiptune loop in A minor (Am–F–C–G ×2): square-wave
arpeggio lead, triangle-wave bass, and noise hats. Every note starts and
ends at zero amplitude on the 8th-note grid, so the loop point is
seamless. It is produced by `generate_gameplay_music.py` in this folder.
